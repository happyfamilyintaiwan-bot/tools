Frequently asked questions

Which eSIM provider is cheapest?

It depends on the destination, trip length and how much data you use. From prices checked in September 2026: for short trips of 3 to 7 days with modest data, Airalo is usually cheapest and Saily is typically about US$1 behind. For 10 days or longer, or around 2GB a day, Yesim 30-day plans are often the lowest. If you are new to Saily, the code ZOEEHA9323 takes US$5 off a first purchase, which usually makes it the cheapest option on short trips.

How much mobile data do I actually use on a trip?

Maps, messaging and looking up restaurants runs about 0.3 to 0.5GB a day. Add social feeds and photo uploads and it is roughly 1GB. Short-form video or video calls push it to about 2GB. Streaming or sharing a hotspot can exceed 4GB. If you are unsure, pick Normal and leave some headroom.

Is unlimited eSIM data really unlimited?

Rarely. Saily gives 5GB a day at full speed before throttling. Airalo is mostly 3GB a day, 5GB in some countries, then 1Mbps. Yesim applies a fair-use policy that may throttle very heavy use. After throttling you can still use maps and messaging, but video will struggle.

How do I use the Saily discount code?

Pick your plan in the Saily app or on the website and enter ZOEEHA9323 at checkout. New customers get US$5 off their first purchase, subject to Saily terms. Every purchase also earns 3% back in Saily credits.

Does my phone support eSIM?

iPhone XS and XR onwards, Google Pixel 3 onwards and most Samsung Galaxy S20 and later models support it. The quickest check: search your phone settings for EID. If a long number appears, you are set. Carrier-locked phones need unlocking first.

Can I still receive SMS on my Taiwan number while using an eSIM?

Yes. Keep your physical SIM active with its data roaming switched off, and run mobile data on the eSIM. You keep receiving texts, including bank and government verification codes, without paying roaming data charges. Incoming SMS is normally free, though calls may still be billed.

When should I install the eSIM?

Install it a day or two before departure while you are on Wi-Fi, then switch on data roaming for that eSIM after you land. Most plans only start counting days once you connect at the destination, so installing early does not waste validity.

Which eSIM should I buy for travelling within Asia from Taiwan?

For a single country, a single-country plan is almost always cheaper than a regional one. Regional plans only pay off when you cross two or more borders in the same trip, for example Hong Kong plus Shenzhen, or several Southeast Asian countries back to back.
