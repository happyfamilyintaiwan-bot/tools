§ 03 — FAQ

Questions

Are these tools free?

Yes. Every tool is free and there is nothing to sign up for. Open the page and start.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

Most tools run entirely in your browser, so things like expense lists stay on your own phone or computer. Features that need the internet, such as exchange rates, say so on the tool page.

Can I add a tool to my home screen?

On iPhone, open the tool in Safari and tap Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen. It will then open like an app.
