HOW TO

How to use these Instagram symbols

  1. Lay out your bio: type three lines in the bio layout tool, pick a frame, and watch the character counter.
  2. Pick symbols: dividers, kaomoji, tiny bio symbols and story decorations all copy with one tap.
  3. Paste in Instagram: edit your profile or story text and paste. For a blank line, paste an invisible line break.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Can I use fancy fonts in my Instagram name?

In the name field, yes. In your username, no. Usernames only accept letters, numbers, periods and underscores, so styled letters and symbols will not paste. The name field takes them, but styled letters are different characters to the system, so people searching your name in plain letters may not find you. Keep your brand or real name in plain text and decorate around it.

How do I add a line break or blank line in my Instagram bio?

Line breaks typed with Enter in the app sometimes disappear, and repeated blank lines are always merged. The most reliable way is to lay out the whole block in the bio layout tool on this page and paste it in one go. Where you want a blank line, paste “Line break” from the invisible characters section.

How long can an Instagram bio be? Do symbols count?

150 characters, and symbols, spaces and line breaks all count. A full divider is about 13, so three of them use a quarter of your bio. Some teary-eyed kaomoji look like five characters but cost 14 to 16, because those eyes are several marks stacked on top of each other.

Why do some symbols turn into boxes in my story?

The phone has no font for that character, so it draws a box instead. The trendy set at the bottom of the story section uses rarer characters such as bows and little fish. Most iPhones show them, but some Android phones show boxes. For something everyone will see, pick from the steady set at the top.

Will bunny text art line up on Instagram?

It may not. Multi-line bunnies need every character to be the same width to line up, and Instagram uses a proportional font, so they shift a little. Two-line bunnies are usually fine; three lines or more drift more. Single-line kaomoji never have this problem.

Can Threads have a different bio from Instagram?

No. Threads uses the same bio field as Instagram, so changing one changes both. Check your layout in both apps.
