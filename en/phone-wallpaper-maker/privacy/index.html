Hiyori Blooms App Privacy Policy

Applies to the iPhone app Hiyori Blooms | Last updated: 28 November 2026

In short: this app collects no personal data. Wallpapers are drawn on your phone, and the ones you make stay on your phone.

What we do not collect

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics, ads or trackers. Nothing about how you use the app is sent to us or anyone else.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts or advertising ID.
  • Wallpapers are drawn on your phone and never uploaded to a server.

Photos access

The app only asks for "Add Photos Only" access, so it can save your wallpaper to Photos. This access can only add photos. The app cannot see or read anything else in your library.

Data on your phone

My Works (the settings and handwriting for each wallpaper) and your last settings live only inside the app. If iCloud Backup is on, they are included in your phone backup, which Apple keeps and we cannot read. Deleting the app deletes them too.

In-app purchases

Plus and seasonal flower packs are paid through Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details. The app only checks with the App Store which items you own, so it can unlock them.

The "Get Today's Wallpaper" shortcut

This Shortcuts action draws today's wallpaper on your phone and hands it to the shortcut you set up. It is not sent anywhere else.

Outside links

The Settings tab has links to email us and to open the Knitting Hiyori website. The website uses cookies for analytics and ads. See the Knitting Hiyori privacy policy.

Children

Children can use the app. We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children under 13, and we use no ads, advertising ID, location or tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions are welcome at newsletters@knittinghiyori.com.
