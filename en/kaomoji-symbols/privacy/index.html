Kaomoji Hiyori App Privacy Policy

Applies to the iPhone app Kaomoji Hiyori and its kaomoji keyboard | Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: this app collects no personal data. Neither the app nor the keyboard goes online, and your saved and recent kaomoji stay on your own phone.

What we do not collect

  • No account. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics, ads or trackers. Nothing about how you use the app is sent to us or anyone else.
  • No microphone, camera, location, contacts or advertising ID.

The kaomoji keyboard

The keyboard does not ask for Full Access, so iOS does not let it go online or send anything outside the keyboard. It only types the kaomoji you tap. It never reads, stores or sends what you type yourself.

Data on your phone

Your saved and recent kaomoji live only inside the app and the keyboard. If iCloud Backup is on, they are included in your phone backup, which Apple keeps and we cannot read. Deleting the app deletes them too.

Outside links

The app has a button that opens your phone Settings. The Knitting Hiyori website uses cookies for analytics and ads. See the Knitting Hiyori privacy policy.

Children

Children can use the app. We collect no personal data from anyone, including children under 13, and use no ads, advertising ID, location or tracking.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions are welcome at newsletters@knittinghiyori.com.
