Kaomoji Generator: Bunny Text Art & 1,152-Face Mixer

When an emoji is too loud, a symbol still speaks.

In short:Bunny text art, a kaomoji face mixer, dividers, invisible characters and fancy fonts. Tap once to copy. Works in Instagram, Threads, X, Discord and anywhere else that takes text.

Short answers: For bunny text art, pick what the bunny holds and tap to copy. For a custom kaomoji, choose eyes and a mouth. 36 × 32 gives 1,152 faces. To keep line breaks on Instagram, paste a Hangul Filler (U+3164) instead of a normal space. An Instagram bio is 150 characters, and one full divider costs about 13 of them.
