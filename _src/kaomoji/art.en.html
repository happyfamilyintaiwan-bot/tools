How to use the kaomoji generator

  1. Pick a section: bunny text art for multi-line art, the face mixer for single-line kaomoji, or jump to symbols from the menu bar.
  2. Tap once to copy: choose what the bunny holds or which eyes to use, then tap. A “Copied” note confirms it.
  3. Paste it in your app: Instagram, Threads, X, Discord or anywhere that takes text. Check the counter in the layout tool before you edit your bio.

Questions people ask

What does kaomoji mean?

Kaomoji is Japanese for “face characters”. Kao (face) plus moji (character). Unlike Western emoticons you read sideways, kaomoji sit upright, like (^_^). The style is commonly traced to 1986 in Japan, and it spread because the eyes carry the emotion, so faces can be rebuilt from parts.

How do I add a line break in my Instagram bio or caption?

Paste an invisible character on the empty line. Instagram removes blank lines and leading spaces, so pressing Enter twice does not survive. Copy “Line break” from the invisible characters section and paste it wherever you want the gap. Laying out the whole block here and pasting it in one go is the most reliable method.

What is an invisible character and why do I need one?

Instagram, Facebook and Threads trim leading whitespace and collapse blank lines, so your carefully spaced caption arrives as a wall. An invisible character looks empty but is not classified as whitespace, so the trimmer skips it. The two that reliably survive are the Hangul Filler (U+3164) and the Braille Blank (U+2800). Plenty of sites hand out a normal full-width space instead, which is exactly why it stops working the moment you paste.

Why do some symbols show up as empty boxes?

Your device does not have a font covering that character, so it draws a placeholder. Often called tofu. It happens most with the Musical Symbols block: that pretty eighth note 𝅘𝅥𝅮 is actually three stacked codepoints and most phones cannot render it. Everything in the music section here is basic notation or emoji, and the trendy sets that can break are labelled.

Do decorative symbols count toward the Instagram character limit?

Yes, and more than you would guess. Instagram and Threads bios are 150 characters, X is 160, and line breaks count too. A full divider is about 13. Some kaomoji are worse: (o̴̶̷᷄ᵕo̴̶̷̥᷅) looks like five characters and costs fourteen, because those watery eyes are one letter with five combining marks stacked on it. The counter in the layout tool shows the real number.

Why do fancy fonts not work with Japanese, Chinese or Korean?

Because they are not fonts. They are Unicode Mathematical Alphanumeric Symbols. A block that only contains A to Z, a to z and 0 to 9. There is no bold や, no script 中, no italic 한. Every converter on the web has this limit; none of them are broken. For CJK text the way to add character is layout and decorative symbols, not letterforms.

Can I use fancy fonts in my Instagram username?

No. Usernames only accept letters, numbers, periods and underscores, so styled letters will not paste. The name field does accept them. Even there, search may not match styled letters to normal ones and screen readers cannot pronounce them, so keep your real name or brand in plain text and decorate around it.

Will the bunny text art line up when I paste it?

Depends where. Multi-line text art needs a monospaced font to align: Discord and most code-style places are fine. Instagram and Threads use proportional fonts, so tall bunnies can drift. Two-line bunnies drift less, and single-line kaomoji are safe everywhere.

Can I have a different bio on Threads and Instagram?

No. Threads reads the same bio field as Instagram and syncs it both ways, so whatever you write has to work in both places.
