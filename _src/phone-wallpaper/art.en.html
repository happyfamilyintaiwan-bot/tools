How to make a phone wallpaper in five steps

  1. Check your phone model: open this page on your phone and it uses your screen resolution. On a computer, pick the phone you want the wallpaper for.
  2. Pick a palette: tap a swatch to change colors and switch between flat and gradient. Want your own colors? Open “Custom colors”.
  3. Pick a symbol and a layout: choose one of 20 symbols, then one big symbol, a pattern, a scattered look, or a gradient with a small symbol. Use the sliders for size and density.
  4. Add a short line (optional): any language works. Choose sans or serif and place it at the top, middle or bottom.
  5. Save to your phone: tap “Save to phone”, choose “Save Image” in the share sheet, then set it as your wallpaper from Photos. Can't decide? Tap “Surprise me”.

Menu names vary a little by system version. Look for “Wallpaper”.

Tips for a clean, minimal wallpaper

Keep the home screen quiet. Your app icons sit on top of it, so lower the density on a pattern or use the gradient layout. Save the bold, single symbol for the lock screen, where only the clock competes with it.

Dark backgrounds are easier on the eyes at night. Midnight, Charcoal and Cherry are the dark palettes.

The bottom is the safest place for text. The clock and widgets take the top, and notifications can cover the middle.

Phone wallpaper maker FAQ

Can I use and share the wallpapers I make?

Yes. Every symbol is drawn by Knitting Hiyori and the palettes are original, so there's no third-party artwork involved. Use them as wallpapers, send them to friends or post them online. Please don't sell the images themselves.

What size should I choose?

Just pick your phone model. The tool exports a PNG at your screen's resolution, for example 1179 × 2556 for iPhone 16. If your model isn't listed, pick the closest size; your phone scales it when you set the wallpaper.

I tapped “Save to phone” but nothing downloaded.

On a phone, a share sheet opens. Choose “Save Image”. If your browser can't share files, the tool shows the image instead: press and hold it, then tap “Add to Photos” or “Download image”.

Can I make it on my computer?

Yes. Pick your phone model, click “Save to phone” to download the PNG, and send it to your phone with AirDrop, a messaging app or cloud storage. Or share the settings link with yourself and open it on your phone.

Will the clock cover my text?

Turn on the clock preview to see roughly where the clock and the bottom buttons sit. It never appears in the saved image. Text set to “Top” is placed below the clock, and “Bottom” is the safest choice.

Can I make a text-only wallpaper?

Yes. Choose “No symbol” and you get a flat or gradient background with just your words.

Is anything uploaded?

No. The wallpaper is drawn in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; the tool only remembers your last settings on this device.

How can a friend make the same wallpaper?

Tap “Share these settings with a friend”. The link opens with the same colors, symbol, layout and text, sized for their own phone.
