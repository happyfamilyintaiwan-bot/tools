Color Palette from Photo: pull the colors out of any picture

In short: upload a photo you love and get its main colors as a palette, with HEX, RGB, HSL and CMYK codes. Drag the dots on the photo to fine-tune, then send it to Procreate, Figma or your CSS. The photo never leaves your browser.
